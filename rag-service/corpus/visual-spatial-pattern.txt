The visual-spatial pattern

WHAT THIS PATTERN MEANS

Reversals and letter swaps were prominent in your writing, or the way your letters were
formed, spaced or sized was inconsistent.

One caveat matters before anything else. In writers under about seven, reversals are a
normal stage of development. On their own they are not an indicator of anything. They
carry weight only when they appear alongside other patterns.

WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE IN WRITING

Mirror-image letters exchanged. The pairs most often swapped are b and d, p and q, m and w,
and n and u. The pairs b and p, d and q, s and z, and g and q are swapped less often, but
they do occur.

Neighbouring letters swapped. "girl" becomes "gril".

Letter formation. Letters started in the wrong place, or built in an unusual order, even
when the finished letter is readable.

Spacing. Words crowded together or pushed too far apart, so the line is hard to read back.

Sizing. Letters that change size within a word or a line, or that do not sit consistently
on the line.

WHAT HELPS

Letter formation and orientation work, with multisensory cues for the confusable pairs.

For each confusable pair, learn one physical cue and use it every single time. Trace the
letter large - in the air, on a rough surface, in sand - while saying its sound aloud. The
movement gives you a second way to tell the pair apart, one that does not depend on
looking.

Take the pairs one at a time. Get b secure on its own before you put b and d side by side.
Practising a confusable pair together too early tends to strengthen the confusion rather
than resolve it.

Proof-reading routines that slow you down. Read your writing back letter by letter, with a
finger or a ruler under the line. The goal is to stop your eye sliding over a reversed
letter - which it will do, because it already knows what you meant to write.

For spacing and sizing, use lined or squared paper, and put one finger down between words
as you write.

HOW TO PRACTISE

Short and frequent beats long and rare.

Formation habits change slowly, so expect this to take weeks rather than days.

Write more slowly than feels natural while a habit is changing. Speed comes back once the
new movement is automatic.
